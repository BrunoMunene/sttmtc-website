Simple Process

How to Apply — 5 Steps

Applying to STTMTC-K is straightforward. Follow these steps to secure your place in the September 2026 intake.

1

Check Requirements

Review the KCSE entry grades and confirm you qualify before applying.

2

Fill Application

Complete the online form below or download and fill the physical form.

3

Pay App. Fee

Pay KSh 1,000 non-refundable fee via M-Pesa or at the Accounts Office.

4

Attend Interview

Shortlisted applicants are called for an assessment and oral interview.

5

Enrol & Report

Successful candidates receive offer letters and report on Orientation Day.

Eligibility

Admission Requirements

For the Community Health Nursing (KRCHN) programme:

  • KCSE Mean Grade C (Plain) or above
  • Grade C (Plain) in Biology and English or Kiswahili
  • Grade C– in Chemistry, Physics, or Mathematics
  • Age 18–35 years at time of application
  • Kenyan citizen with valid ID or Birth Certificate
  • Medical fitness certificate from a registered facility

📎 Documents to Submit

  • Original KCSE Certificate or Result Slip
  • National ID or Birth Certificate (certified copy)
  • 2 recent passport-size photographs
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Certificate of Good Conduct (Police Clearance)
  • Application fee receipt (KSh 1,000)
