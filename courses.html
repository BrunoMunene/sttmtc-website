💉 Community Health Nursing
Diploma Programme

Choose Your Healthcare Path

STTMTC-K offers one focused, deeply resourced nursing pathway — blending academic excellence with daily clinical practice in our Level 5 hospital.

Diploma · 3½ Years · KRCHN

Community Health Nursing

Regulated by the Nursing Council of Kenya (NCK) · Examined by KNEC

Master the art of healing and community wellness through intensive clinical placements in one of Kenya's most active Level 5 teaching hospitals.

📋 Entry Requirements

  • KCSE Mean Grade C (Plain) or above
  • C (Plain) in Biology & English or Kiswahili
  • C– in Chemistry, Physics, or Mathematics

🏥 Clinical Rotations Include

  • General medicine, surgery & maternity wards
  • Paediatrics, ICU & theatre rotations
  • Community health outreach programmes

🎓 Career Outcomes

  • Registered Community Health Nurse (KRCHN)
  • Public health officer in government facilities
  • Maternal & child health specialist
  • NGO and community health programmes

Where Our Graduates Go

Career Outcomes

STTMTC-K nursing graduates are sought after across Kenya's healthcare sector — in public hospitals, private facilities, NGOs, research institutions, and community health programmes.

💉

Nursing Graduates

  • Registered Community Health Nurse (KRCHN)
  • Public hospitals — county & national level
  • Private clinics and mission hospitals
  • Maternal & child health specialist roles
  • NGO community health programmes
  • International nursing migration pathways
🌿

Community & Public Health

  • County health department postings
  • Community health centre management
  • Dispensary and health post roles
  • USAID, WHO, UNICEF health programmes
  • School health and occupational nursing
📚

Further Studies

  • Upgrade to BSc Nursing (KU, UoN, MKU)
  • Specialise — ICU, Theatre, Oncology
  • Midwifery specialisation
  • Public health & epidemiology postgraduate
  • Nursing education & tutorship
