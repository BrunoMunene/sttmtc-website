Frequently Asked

Common Questions

What are the minimum KCSE grades for the nursing programme?
You need a KCSE mean grade of C (Plain) or above, with at least C in Biology and English or Kiswahili, and C– in Mathematics or Chemistry.
When is the next intake and how many spaces are available?
The next intake is September 2026. There are 80 spaces available and applications close on August 15, 2026. We recommend applying early.
How much are the total fees for the nursing programme?
Total fees are approximately KSh 90,000 per year (Year 1), covering tuition, clinical attachment, examination, and deposits. Download the full fee structure here.
Is HELB available for students at STTMTC-K?
Yes. Qualifying Kenyan students can apply for HELB (Higher Education Loans Board) loans. Contact our Finance Office for guidance on the HELB application process.
Is accommodation available on campus?
Yes. We have on-campus hostels within the St Theresa Mission Hospital compound — separate male and female facilities with 24-hour security. Hostel fees are separate from tuition.
What documents do I need to apply?
You need your KCSE certificate or result slip, National ID or Birth Certificate (certified copy), 2 passport photos, a medical fitness certificate, Certificate of Good Conduct, and proof of application fee payment (KSh 1,000).
How do I access the Student Portal?
Current students can access the portal at the Student Portal link. Your login credentials are issued by the ICT department upon enrolment. For access issues, contact ict@sttheresamedcollege.co.ke.
Is STTMTC-K accredited and government recognised?
Yes. Our KRCHN programme is fully accredited by the Kenya Nursing Council (KNC) and examined by KNEC. We are also registered with the TVET Authority of Kenya.
