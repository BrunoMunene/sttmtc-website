Est. 1967 · Kiirua, Meru County

More Than a College.
A Calling to Serve.

St Theresa Medical Training College (STTMTC-K) is a constituent training institution of St Theresa Mission Hospital — one of Kenya's most respected faith-based healthcare facilities. Nestled in the cool highlands of Kiirua, Meru County, our college has been forming compassionate, competent healthcare professionals since 1967.

We believe that medical training is not merely an academic exercise — it is a vocation. Our students do not just learn about healthcare; they live it every day, rotating through real wards, serving real patients, and being mentored by experienced clinicians within a Level 5 teaching hospital.

Our flagship programme — the Kenya Registered Community Health Nurse (KRCHN) — is fully accredited by the Kenya Nursing Council and examined by KNEC, producing graduates who are recognised, sought-after, and ready to serve from day one.

🎯 Our Vision

To be the leading faith-based medical training college in East Africa, producing healthcare professionals of the highest calibre.

🏥 Our Mission

To nurture excellence in healthcare education through quality training, compassionate values, and unwavering commitment to community service.

Our Story

A Legacy of Healthcare Education

From a small nursing school to one of Kenya's respected medical training colleges — our journey spans over five decades.

1967

Foundation

St Theresa Medical Training College is established by the Little Sisters of St. Therese as a nursing school within the St Theresa Mission Hospital, Kiirua.

1990

KNC Recognition

The Kenya Nursing Council formally accredits the KRCHN diploma programme, establishing our graduates as fully registered community health nurses.

2015

TVET Registration

STTMTC-K registers with the Technical and Vocational Education and Training Authority of Kenya, aligning with national education policy frameworks.

2026

ISO 15189 Laboratory Accreditation

St Theresa Mission Hospital Laboratory achieves KENAS ISO 15189:2012 international accreditation — one of Kenya's first mission hospital labs to reach this standard. Our students now train in a globally recognised facility.

From the Principal's Desk

A Word From
Our Principal

"

Welcome to St Theresa Medical Training College — a place where healthcare is not just a profession, but a calling. For over five decades, this institution has stood at the intersection of faith, service, and academic excellence, producing nurses who carry our values into communities across Kenya and beyond.

Our training model is unique: students don't merely observe healthcare — they participate in it. Embedded within a Level 5 teaching hospital, every lecture is reinforced by real clinical experience, every concept by actual patient interaction. This is the STTMTC-K difference.

We invite you to join a community dedicated to excellence, compassion, and lifelong service. Whether you are a prospective student, a parent, or a partner institution — you are welcome here.

Sr. Annrita Wanjiru Mwangi
