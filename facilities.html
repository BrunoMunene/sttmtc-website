Campus Resources

Our Training Environments

Explore the world-class facilities where our students grow into confident, competent medical professionals.

STMH-K Level 5 Hospital

A fully-operational Level 5 Teaching & Referral facility serving as our primary clinical training ground. Students access real wards, theatres, and ICUs daily.

ISO 15189 Accredited Lab

KENAS-certified to international standards. Our medical laboratory is one of a handful in Kenya to hold ISO 15189:2012 accreditation — ensuring world-class training.

Medical Library & Resource Centre

An extensive collection of medical literature, research journals, and high-speed digital research terminals for academic excellence.

On-Campus Hostels

Safe, comfortable, and spiritually nurturing student residences within the secure campus perimeter. Fully catered and supervised.

Unique Advantage

Level 5 Hospital Integration

St Theresa Medical Training College is uniquely positioned within the St Theresa Mission Hospital complex, offering a seamless training ecosystem where students move from classroom to clinical environment in minutes — not semesters.

  • Direct access to ISO 15189:2012 Accredited Laboratories (KENAS certified)
  • Real-time exposure to complex surgical, medical & obstetric cases
  • Mentorship from the Hospital's multidisciplinary team of specialists
  • Priority clinical rotations in ICU, Renal, Theatre & Maternity units
  • Community health outreach and field service learning programs
More on Campus

Everything You Need to Succeed

Beyond the wards and labs, our campus is designed to support students academically, socially, and spiritually.

🧪

Skills Simulation Lab

Advanced mannequins, IV trainers, and simulation tools for hands-on clinical procedure practice before real-world ward placement.

💻

ICT & Computer Lab

Modern workstations with internet access, EMR software training platforms, and digital health information systems.

🍽️

Student Cafeteria

Affordable, nutritious meals prepared fresh daily within the campus compound. Special dietary needs accommodated.

Football pitch, volleyball courts, and open recreation areas ensuring students stay physically active and mentally balanced.

Digital Library Access

Subscriptions to online medical journals, HINARI database access, and a growing collection of physical medical textbooks.

Chapel & Spiritual Formation

A serene on-campus chapel for prayer, reflection, and faith formation — central to our holistic education philosophy.

Transport Links

Secure Campus

Knowledge Hub

Medical Library & Resource Centre

Our medical library provides students with the reference materials and research tools they need to excel academically and clinically.

  • Over 2,000 medical textbooks and reference volumes
  • Subscriptions to peer-reviewed journals via HINARI
  • Dedicated quiet study rooms and group discussion spaces
  • High-speed internet terminals for digital research
  • Photocopying and printing services on-site
Student Residence

On-Campus Student Hostels

Safe, comfortable, and spiritually nurturing accommodation within the secure Mission Hospital campus perimeter.

Our hostels are designed to complement the academic journey — clean, well-maintained rooms with study desks, reliable water supply, and pastoral care from the Little Sisters of St. Therese.

  • Separate male and female hostels
  • 24-hour security and campus curfew
  • Common room with TV and recreation facilities
  • On-site laundry facilities
  • Spiritual direction and pastoral support available
