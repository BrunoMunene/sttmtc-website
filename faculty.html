Our Approach

Teaching Philosophy

Every STTMTC-K educator is guided by a shared set of principles that put student growth, patient safety, and community service at the centre of everything we do.

🛏️

Bedside Teaching

Students learn best at the bedside. Every ward rotation is a teaching moment — faculty accompany students into wards, not just classrooms.

🔬

Lab-Led Learning

Our ISO-certified laboratory is not just a resource — it is a daily classroom. Students run real tests under qualified technologists from Year 1.

🤝

Mentorship First

Every student is assigned a clinical mentor for the duration of their programme. The mentor relationship is the cornerstone of STTMTC-K training.

✝️

Faith-Based Formation

Professional excellence is inseparable from character. We train nurses who are not only competent but compassionate — shaped by the values of St. Therese.

🌿

Community Outreach

Twice a year, students participate in community health outreach in surrounding villages — applying public health knowledge in real community settings.

📊

Standards-Driven Assessment

All assessments align with KNEC and Kenya Nursing Council standards, ensuring students are examination-ready and career-ready at the same time.
